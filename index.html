Sam Black's Commander Cube Rules

Draft: 3 packs of 20 cards, pick 2 per pick. All players draft simultaneously in a pod.


Packs pass: left for pack 1, right for pack 2, left for pack 3.


Deck: 60 cards, following Commander color identity rules. Commander is not included in the 60.


Commander: Drafted from packs as normal cards. Choose one after the draft. ★ cards are legendary and can be commanders.


Command Tower: Every player gets one free Command Tower added to their pool after the draft.


Mono-color splash: If your commander is mono-color, you may choose a second color and include up to 6 spells of that color plus any lands for it.


Play: 4-player free-for-all. All normal Commander rules apply except deck is 60 cards.
